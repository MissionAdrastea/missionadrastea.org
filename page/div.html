What we are working on. Current projects, division by division.

Research

Health & Physiology

  • Hibernation and torpor in spaceflight

    A narrative review of what hibernation and torpor could mean for women.

  • Female-focused spaceflight frameworks

    A flight planning framework built around women’s health.

  • Analog research Flagship

    Our 2029 analog research project, bringing Health & Physiology and Bioengineering & Innovation together.

  • Cycle tracking study

    Expanded cycle-tracking research using 50 to 100 smart rings.

  • Neuroscience and women’s health

    A collaboration spanning reproductive health and brain science.

Prototyping

Bioengineering & Innovation

  • Menstrual cups in aerospace

    A microgravity menstrual cup in medical-grade silicone, with material validation and rheological blood analogs.

  • Satellite mapping of period poverty

    Using existing satellite data to find gaps in menstrual hygiene access and guide nonprofit supply chains.

Advocacy

Policy, Ethics & Society

  • Menstrual health and algorithmic transparency

    Advocating for legislation that advances health literacy on social media platforms and raises awareness of misinformation.

  • Workshops

    Advocating for under-18 involvement in space policy and decision-making.

  • Partnerships

    Planned summer advocacy for women’s health research and youth participation in policy.

  • White papers

    The Period as an Underlying Covariate for Analog Missions: menstrual cycle considerations often overlooked in analog research.
