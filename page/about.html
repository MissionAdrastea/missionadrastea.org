Ad astra, for all.

Closing the sex gap in aerospace.

Adrastea is a youth-led accelerator and collaboration hub dedicated to advancing women in space through research and advocacy. We serve all women in the space industry, whether they are doctors, engineers, designers, or female astronauts, with the aim of prioritizing, and advocating for their prioritization. We strive for an equitable future for human spaceflight.

Our mission

Our goal is not just to identify gaps, but to build the research, projects, and partnerships that address them.

  1. 1

    Ensure women are not an afterthought in space exploration

  2. 2

    Change how the industry attends to women’s health

  3. 3
