“ad astra, for all.”

Adrastea is an accelerator and collaboration hub dedicated to advancing women in space through research and advocacy. We serve all women in the space industry, whether they are doctors, engineers, designers, or female astronauts, with the aim of prioritizing, and advocating for their prioritization. We strive for an equitable future for human spaceflight.

Our Mission

1

Ensure women are not an afterthought in space exploration

2

Change how the industry attends to women’s health

3

Build a leading hub for women’s health in space
