Why Adrastea? Five things that set us apart.

Research-driven. Conducting original research, not just awareness or networking.

Women across the lifespan. Addressing women’s health beyond student populations.

Youth-led, built to scale. Developing a pipeline of younger fellows as founders progress.

Research made accessible. Translating findings through social media, not just paywalled publications.

A unique intersection. Women’s health and space medicine, approached through science, engineering, policy, and media.

Women’s health in space calls for more than one kind of expertise. Adrastea brings research, engineering, policy, and communication into the same conversation. Our goal is not just to identify gaps, but to build the research, projects, and partnerships that address them.

How our work comes together

Ask better questions

Investigate the health and human-factors questions that shape women’s ability to access and thrive in space.

Build useful ideas

Explore technology and design approaches that respond to real needs in spaceflight and beyond.

Move knowledge forward

Translate evidence into policy, advocacy, and clear communication so more people can take part in the work.

Three divisions, one orbit.

Adrastea functions across three core research divisions and a media team, bringing young researchers together with senior scientists, engineers, and policymakers.

Research division

Health & Physiology

We study how the female body uniquely responds to space, from reproductive and hormonal health to radiation, neuroscience, and microgravity adaptation.
