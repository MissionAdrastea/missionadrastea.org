Why Adrastea? Five things that set us apart.

  • Research-driven.

    Conducting original research, not just raising awareness or networking.

  • Women across the lifespan.

    Addressing women’s health at every age, not only among students.

  • Youth-led, built to scale.

    Bringing in younger fellows as today’s leaders move on.

  • Research made accessible.

    Translating findings through social media, not just paywalled publications.

  • A unique intersection.

    Women’s health and space medicine, approached through science, engineering, policy, and media.
